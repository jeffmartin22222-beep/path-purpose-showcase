## Scientific teaching goal
A protein-containing secretory vesicle approaches and docks at the plasma membrane. **Contact does not itself release cargo**: fusion of the vesicle and plasma membranes creates a continuous passage, enabling cargo to leave the cell by exocytosis. This animation is a conceptual illustration, **not** a molecular dynamics simulation. The Golgi can sort/package secretory proteins; do not imply it synthesizes them or that all proteins are exported. Cargo movement, pore morphology and timing are simplified. Do not show cargo crossing an intact lipid bilayer.

## Student checkpoint contract

Still enclosed in the vesicle** (correct); **B. Already outside the cell** (incorrect). Give immediate deterministic feedback: A — *Correct. Docking brings the membranes together, but cargo cannot exit through an unopened passage.* B — *Not yet. Contact alone does not create an exit; look for membrane fusion and an open connection.* Allow unlimited attempts and a rewatch of 13–18 s without losing the original prediction.
