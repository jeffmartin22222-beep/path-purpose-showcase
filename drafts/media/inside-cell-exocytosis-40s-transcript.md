# Inside the Cell — original narration transcript (draft)

A cell is preparing to send a protein beyond its boundary. Inside, a transport vesicle carries that cargo toward the surface. Its membrane keeps the protein separate from the cytoplasm. Now it reaches the plasma membrane. But touching is not enough. Predict: can the protein escape before the membranes fuse? Watch: the membranes merge, forming a passage to the outside. Only then can the enclosed cargo move into the space beyond the cell. This is exocytosis: release through membrane fusion. What evidence showed that contact alone was not release?
