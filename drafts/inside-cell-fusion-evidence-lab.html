What the model shows

Before fusion, the protein remains enclosed by the vesicle membrane. Fusion creates a continuous passage to the extracellular space, allowing cargo to be released. This process is called exocytosis.

Model limitation: membrane remodeling and molecular fusion machinery are simplified. This is not a quantitative simulation of real fusion kinetics.
